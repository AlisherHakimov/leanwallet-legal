Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your financial data is stored locally on your device. LeanWallet does not upload your transactions to any server. To keep your data safe, use the in-app backup/export feature regularly.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Settings → Premium and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I add the home screen widget?

Long-press your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for LeanWallet, and choose a widget size. Detailed steps are also available in the app under Settings → Home Screen Widget.

Receipt scanning isn't working — what can I do?

Make sure LeanWallet has camera permission (iOS Settings → LeanWallet → Camera), and hold the receipt flat under good lighting. You can also pick a receipt photo from your gallery instead.

Which currencies are supported?

LeanWallet supports 150+ currencies with automatic exchange-rate conversion. Change your main currency in Settings → Currency.

How do I delete my data?

Since all data lives on your device, deleting the app removes all data. You can also erase everything from within the app via Settings → Data → Delete All Data.
